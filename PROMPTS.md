# Image prompts

The five files in `public/images/` are abstract placeholders generated by
`npm run placeholders`. Replace each with a **real photograph** of the centre,
keeping the exact filename — the paths are referenced from `lib/site.ts`. If a
photograph is not available yet, the prompts below produce a stand-in; write
them in English, image models lose detail in translation.

Art direction matches `design.md`: warm off-white `#faf6f2`, a soft blush
`#fdeae2`, warm brown-black `#291f19`, and one terracotta-coral accent
`#9c4535`. Nothing saturated, nothing cold.

**House style — append to every prompt:**

> Editorial interior and lifestyle photography, natural soft daylight, warm
> off-white walls, shallow depth of field, calm and uncluttered, muted warm
> palette of off-white, blush and pale wood with a single terracotta accent,
> Mediterranean light, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no visible brand
> names, photorealistic, 35mm lens look, gentle contrast.

**Children.** Where a child appears they must look relaxed and engaged: no
tears, no restraint, no medical equipment. A parent's face should never be
identifiable. Prefer hands, backs of heads, or rooms without people.

> **Note.** This is a portrait of a real, named professional. Whatever image is

> with picture cards, the room) instead of a generated face.

## After swapping the files

- Resize to about 1400 px on the long edge.
- Convert to WebP (quality 82) and change the extensions in `lib/site.ts`
  (`spaceImages`, `therapistImage`) from `.png` to `.webp`.
- `npm run build` and check every page.

To regenerate the placeholders at any point: `npm run placeholders`.
